Human-Aware AI (HAAI) Research Group

We’re a research group within the ISCAS HCI Lab. We study how people and AI understand each other and adapt as they work together.

Vision

For an AI system to be compatible with a person, it needs to recognize that person’s goals and notice when they change. The person, in turn, needs enough insight into the system to question its advice, correct its assumptions, or take over. We study both sides of that relationship.

In our current projects, we ask how a coding agent can recognize a change of intent, how a person can oversee its work, and what the agent should remember the next time they work together.
