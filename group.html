Human-Aware AI (HAAI) Research Group

We’re a research group within the ISCAS HCI Lab. We study how people and AI understand each other and adapt as they work together.

Vision

AI should not only be intelligent. It should be intelligent about us.

We envision a future where humans and AI develop a deeper understanding of one another, enabling collaboration that is more adaptive, trustworthy, and genuinely empowering. This calls for a shift from building AI that simply performs tasks to building AI that understands human intentions, respects individual differences, and evolves through interaction. At the same time, humans must be able to understand, question, and shape the AI systems they work with.

Our current projects bring this vision to life through two intertwined research directions: inventing new mechanisms for human–AI collaboration and developing computational models that make AI more human-aware. Ultimately, we seek to move beyond AI that works for humans toward AI that thinks, learns, and grows with them.
